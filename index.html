💫 Made for Children. Helpful for Parents.

MultipliNova makes times tables practice feel like an adventure while giving parents a clear picture of their child's learning.

Choose the times tables to practise, set question timers, review progress and help children develop faster, more confident recall.

The Parents Dashboard provides progress information, mastery grids and a weekly summary. Children can also practise at their own pace without a time limit.

🔒 Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

This privacy policy explains how Xtables: MultipliNova handles information when you use the app.

Information stored on your device

Player profiles, names, game progress, scores, stars, achievements and customisation choices are stored locally on the device. This information is used to provide the game's features and retain player progress.

Information sent to external servers

MultipliNova does not send player profiles or game progress to external servers. The app does not require children to create an online account.

Advertising and tracking

MultipliNova does not use player information for targeted advertising or tracking.

Sharing information

We do not sell or share locally stored player profiles or game progress with third parties.

Deleting local information

Player information is stored on the device. Removing the app may delete its locally stored information, subject to the device's storage and backup settings.

Contacting support

If you email us for support, we receive the information you choose to include in your message, such as your email address and the details of your enquiry. We use this information to respond to your request.

Children's privacy

MultipliNova is designed for children's educational use. Children do not need to provide contact details or create an online account to play.

Contact

For privacy questions, email multiplinovasupport@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the app or its data practices change. The updated policy will be published on this page.
